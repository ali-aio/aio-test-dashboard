No pick made here on purpose — you said surprise you, not pick for you. My actual lean is C for the detail view + D for the list, used together (D's table row click opens a C-style history panel below it): D answers "what does QA already know how to read" and C answers "the specific thing you just asked for" (battery-cycle testing), without committing to the all-dark ops-room feel of A or the weaker cross-device comparison of B.
